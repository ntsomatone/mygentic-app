The Context Architecture

Six dimensions. Three depth layers. One coherent you.

Every dimension captured at three depths. Surface facts you'd put on a résumé. The beliefs and frameworks underneath. The preferences that shape how you actually live. 18 cells. One model. Sage builds the deeper layers most AI tools never see.

Facts.

Roles, locations, demographics, lifestyle. The verifiable surface of who you are.

Layer 02

How you think

Values, motivations, decision patterns, communication style. The lens you see the world through.

Layer 03

Preferences.

How you live

Brand affinities, dietary choices, aesthetic taste, tone of voice. The texture of your daily life.
